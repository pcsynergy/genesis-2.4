# Symphony 5.5 — Scientific and Historical Evidence Register (initial)

**Source:** Pablo Salvador Cardenas, `sysmphony 5.5 (1).docx` (346 pages)  
**Status:** Initial triage, not a full peer review.  
**Purpose:** Separate manuscript statements that can be checked against evidence from metaphor, interpretation, model assumptions, and speculative hypotheses.

## Status labels

- **Established background:** supported by accepted scientific or historical sources, subject to precise wording.
- **Model assumption:** a value, simplification, or rule selected for a model.
- **Mathematical result:** follows from specified equations under stated conditions.
- **Interpretation:** philosophical, theological, symbolic, or literary meaning.
- **Speculative hypothesis:** proposed explanation not established by empirical evidence.
- **Needs verification:** claim is specific enough to check, but supporting references or accurate wording are not yet established.

## Required evidence workflow

For each claim promoted beyond this initial register:

1. Quote or summarize the exact manuscript claim and retain its page anchor.
2. Identify the discipline and the narrow proposition being asserted.
3. Find primary or authoritative sources appropriate to that discipline.
4. Record whether the source supports, qualifies, contradicts, or does not address the claim.
5. Separate factual corrections from the author's intended metaphor or theological position.
6. Record a confidence/status note and the date of review.
7. Only then decide whether a code model is warranted.

## Priority verification queues

### Biology and neuroscience
- DNA versus RNA terminology and base-pair descriptions.
- Human chromosome count and what the manuscript means by “twenty-three.”
- Embryonic germ layers, neural tube formation, neuronal migration, cortical development, and white matter.
- Which phenomena can be represented in a GENESIS neuronal model, and which require developmental biology models outside GENESIS.

### Cosmology and physics
- Distinguish musical/harmonic metaphor from measured physical oscillations.
- Treat Dark Bang or paired-expansion ideas as speculative until an action, field content, covariant equations, stability analysis, and a discriminating observable are supplied.
- Do not identify the OTFT scalar (T) with physical time merely because it is called a clock field.
- Existing FLRW experiments use standard Einstein gravity with a chosen two-scalar matter ansatz; numerical consistency is not observational validation.

### History and cultural astronomy
- Verify archaeological sites and dates individually.
- Verify claimed celestial alignments using site-specific data and uncertainty.
- Preserve the names, histories, and intellectual agency of the specific Indigenous peoples discussed.
- Distinguish archaeological evidence from contemporary spiritual interpretation.

### Philosophy and theology
- Check primary texts and scholarly sources for historical claims about named philosophers and traditions.
- Label theological propositions as theological commitments or interpretations rather than empirical findings.
- Preserve the manuscript's interfaith intent while avoiding the claim that different traditions are identical.

## Scope limitation

This register is an initial triage based on the manuscript's extracted passages and visible page anchors. It is not yet a line-by-line fact-check of all 346 pages. A claim should not be marked “verified” until its specific evidence and source are recorded.
