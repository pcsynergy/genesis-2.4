# Unified Potential Theory — Foundational Specification 0.1

**Author:** Pablo Salvador Cardenas  
**Project:** GENESIS 2.4 / One Time Field research foundation  
**Status:** Conceptual and mathematical research framework; not an empirically established physical theory.

## 1. Central proposition

Unified Potential Theory (UPT) is the overarching framework for investigating how differentiated existence may arise from a more fundamental domain of possibility. Its foundational ordering is:

> **The Potential precedes the One.**

The One is not merely the integer one. It represents the first coherent unity or relational whole within which distinctions can be expressed. The developmental sequence is:

\[
\boxed{\mathcal P\to 1\to 2\to N\to\mathcal A\to\mathcal C\to\mathcal P'}
\]

Potential gives rise to unity (One), distinction (Both), differentiation (Many), interconnected totality (All), creation/becoming, and renewed potential. This is an ontological and organizational hypothesis, not a time-ordered physical process already demonstrated by the cosmological simulations.

**Potential precedes distinction; distinction gives rise to relationship; relationship enables interaction; interaction expresses actuality; actuality continues through becoming and generates new potential.**

## 2. Three meanings that must remain distinct

1. **Ontological Potential** \(\mathcal P\): the domain of admissible possibilities or capacities from which configurations may be actualized. It is not automatically a scalar field, an energy density, or a number.
2. **Model potential energy** \(V(\Psi)\): a scalar function in a specified physical model, with units and dynamics fixed by that model. It is one mathematical representation of stored interaction energy, not the whole ontological meaning of Potential.
3. **Actualized state** \(\Psi\): a selected configuration of fields, relations, and observables within a model.

This distinction prevents a category error: the scalar potential-energy term in a Lagrangian is not, by itself, the foundational Potential of UPT.

## 3. Primitive structure

A general UPT model is represented by
\[
\mathfrak U=(\Omega,\Psi,\mathcal R,\mathcal I,\mathcal E,\mathcal M,\mathcal X),
\]
where \(\Omega\) is the space of admissible possibilities; \(\Psi\in\Omega\) is a state; \(\mathcal R\) is relational structure; \(\mathcal I\) is an interaction rule or operator; \(\mathcal E\) denotes energetic quantities and their transfer or conservation laws; \(\mathcal M\) denotes organized material/field configurations; and \(\mathcal X\) denotes records, measurements, or experiences through which changes are registered.

This tuple is a conceptual architecture. It does not assert that every component is an independent substance, nor that experience is required for physical existence. The observation/experience component distinguishes observer-accessible records from the underlying model state.

A model supplies an evolution law, for example,
\[
\frac{d\Psi}{d\lambda}=\mathcal F(\Psi,\mathcal R,\mathcal I;\theta),
\]
where \(\lambda\) is a declared evolution parameter and \(\theta\) collects model parameters. The function \(\mathcal F\) must be specified for each theory. UPT alone does not determine it.

## 4. Potential, One, Both, Many, All

- **Potential:** the domain of admissible configurations. A mathematical implementation must define its configuration space, constraints, topology, and allowed transformations.
- **One:** an integrated structure in which components can be described within a common framework: a shared state space, action, global field configuration, or connected relational system. It is not a claim that all known interactions have already been unified.
- **Both:** the appearance of distinguishable components and the relations between them. A relation may be represented by a coupling, adjacency, correlation, constraint, or interaction term.
- **Many:** a collection of differentiated states, modes, fields, particles, or network nodes. Their behavior depends on interaction rules and boundary conditions.
- **All:** the complete relational system considered by a model, including its components, interactions, constraints, and boundary conditions. It is not proof that a model describes the entire physical universe.
- **Becoming and renewed Potential:** interactions can change the accessible configuration space or create new effective degrees of freedom. The prime in \(\mathcal P'\) indicates a changed domain of possibilities, not creation from nothing.

## 5. Potential and energy: two explicit hypotheses

UPT preserves an open but disciplined question: is energy wholly an expression of potential, is energy itself co-fundamental, or does the answer depend on the level of description?

- **H1 — Potential-first:** potential is primitive; energetic quantities arise after admissible configurations and interactions are specified.
- **H2 — Co-fundamental potential-energy structure:** potential and energy are inseparable aspects of a deeper relational structure, with energy describing measurable capacity for change.

These hypotheses are not interchangeable and should not be silently blended. The mathematical model must state which it adopts. In either case, measured energy remains subject to the conservation laws and definitions of the physical theory being used.

For a conventional field model,
\[
S=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\left[\mathcal L_{\rm gravity}+\mathcal L_{\rm fields}+\mathcal L_{\rm matter}+\mathcal L_{\rm interaction}\right].
\]
The Lagrangian potential-energy function \(V(\Psi)\) enters the model's dynamics. It is not automatically identical to the full UPT domain \(\mathcal P\). The action, symmetries, field content, and boundary conditions determine which configurations are dynamically allowed.

## 6. Relationship to One Time Field Theory

UPT is the overarching ontology and organizing framework. One Time Field Theory (OTFT) is a focused research program asking how temporal description, change, and relational ordering should be represented within such a framework.

UPT does not by itself prove that time emerges, nor does it identify a scalar field with physical time. OTFT must independently define clocks, observables, coordinate transformations, and the conditions under which a relational time variable can be compared with measured proper time.

In the current cosmological prototype, the symbol T denotes a real scalar matter field. It evolves with respect to FLRW coordinate time and contributes to the stress-energy tensor. It is not identified with proper time or with ontological Potential.

## 8. Requirements for physical models

Every proposed UPT physical model must state its configuration space and degrees of freedom; which variables represent potential, actualized states, relations, and observables; its action or evolution law; units and boundary conditions; symmetries and conservation laws; limiting behavior relative to established theories; at least one quantitative distinguishing prediction; numerical convergence and stability tests; and an operational measurement protocol.

A conceptual sequence is not a substitute for a field equation. A simulation is not evidence for a metaphysical premise merely because its variables can be given that interpretation. A physical claim requires a well-defined observable and a reproducible comparison with data.

## 9. Current status

UPT is integrated as the conceptual and formal umbrella for the GENESIS research layer. Existing network, action, Lorentz-covariant scalar, stress-energy, FLRW, and cosmological-evolution modules remain mathematical implementations of particular models. They are not reinterpreted as proof of UPT. Their equations and verified results remain unchanged unless a separately specified UPT-derived physical model is proposed and tested.

**Foundational statement:** The Potential precedes the One; the One permits distinction; distinction establishes relationship; relationship enables interaction; interaction expresses actuality; and becoming opens new potential.
